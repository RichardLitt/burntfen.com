Yesterday, my low points lasted for hours. I figured it was due to lack of sleep and residual stress. So I went to bed early, almost immediately after writing the last entry. I thought I would try something new. Before reading the next chapter in *A Song of Achilles* – a delightful book from the viewpoint of Patroclus, although I do not like at all how the author appears to be imbuing her characters with anachronistic emotions based on 21st century morality – I rolled up some ear plugs and inserted them, set the aircon on as a noise machine, and got my sleeping mask ready. 

I slept 10.5 hours. 

So, that worked. 

Woke up refreshed. Good work today on Curioss.org, which now has better author tracking for the blog. And fixed a couple of bugs on my NZTCS bot. And did some other small tasks here and there that needed to get done. I prepped for the next few weeks of class. 

I ordered new glasses, and booked a dentist appointment for tomorrow. Now going to sleep again, and hoping to bird tomorrow morning. 

Sleep helps.
