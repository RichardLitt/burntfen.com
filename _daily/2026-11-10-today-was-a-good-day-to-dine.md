Today was an excellent day. I started by grumbling at my alarm clock and self in general at 5:07 for a team meeting where no one else showed up – but once I was awake, I figured I'd get to work. I closed up some threads I had left open the previous evening - [JOSS](https://joss.theoj.org/) reviews, mainly. I finished the first iteration of [dependency-cite](https://github.com/RichardLitt/dependency-cite/), which you can use to find any direct dependencies of a Python package which have explicitly asked in their repository to be cited. I suggested a slough of citations for one of the papers I'm editing based off of it. I figure that if a paper about software doesn't cite software that it uses which has asked people to cite them, I don't know what papers will. I also made a [PR](https://github.com/openjournals/buffy/pull/138) to `buffy`, the tool that JOSS editors use, to make it a bit more NLP-friendly. 

I wrote emails, I wrote yesterday's update, I opened my thesis file and did some work in it. All before breakfast. I don't understand what was different today, but it kept coming.

Mazer Rackham from [Ender's Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ender%27s_Game) urged Ender to "find joy in the interstices of your work". I think of this quote often, mostly after having found joy through working, and mostly not before I start. When I am working on what I do, I am generally happy. So it was.

I wrote a 1000-word blog post for a journal I published in, which I hope makes it through editing, at which point I'll share it. I tried to lock down some more funding for the Sustain Podcast from a major funder I am working with. I failed, and talked to Shinichi about *Budytes taivana* again, but only for a little bit. I worked out – the gym is apparently packed by 10:00am, so I'll have to go earlier tomorrow if I want to go at all. I worked on client work. 

I did something else new - I got three packages in the mail. I now have a much brighter new headlamp for herping, two shirts which are definitely not L, and a new pair of pants (4XL) which fits almost perfectly. I've been wearing them since. Ordering online in China works, and is much quicker than New Zealand or Vermont.  

I've spent the last five hours making slides for tomorrow. It's tough work. My goal tomorrow is to speak as slowly as possible. I'll try to channel [the late great Sir Patrick Stewart](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hA7lv1SDzno). 

One of the best parts of the day was a sesame bun at breakfast. It was delicious. I went back a few hours later looking for more, fruitlessly, bunlessly. Still - today was a good day to dine.
