I went to the dentist yesterday. I've been having a sensitive tooth for the past couple of weeks. The recommended one from one of the staff members here booked next-day appointments. Communication was mostly through translator apps, and sometimes confusing: No, I don't have a mouth uncle, I don't even know what that means – Oh. Ulcer. No, I don't have one of those, either. But after an hour of cleaning and an inspection by two dentists, I was told I don't have a cavity, and generally to keep doing what I've been doing. My teeth looked as sparkly as the bill: 99 yuan, which is around $15. Unbelievably cheap. 

On the way there, I stopped by a local wetland, and found a few new birds for China, and a new lifer – Gray-headed Swamphen, which was recently split out from the pūkeko of New Zealand. The wetland was overdeveloped, but did have some nice environments for birds. I worry about the development and destruction of natural swamp land, here. 

I also went to a coffee shop in the city I like, which my friend introduced me to. It was nice to listen to jazz and just sit somewhere else than my linoleum room for a change. I should go there more. 

I may have had too much coffee; I haven't slept well the last two days. Today I tried to take it easy, as I tend to work Sunday in prep for class. I read a book. I edited a paper. I worked out. I didn't have any morning coffee, as I was told to wait 24 hours to avoid staining my teeth. I made it 22. I think I'll probably be fine. 

The highlight of the last two days was the cab ride home from the dentist, where the driver was animated and patient. We managed to talk for twenty minutes about languages, including Amoy Hokkien, which he spoke. He was amazing I knew as much Mandarin as I knew, which isn't much. I think the bar for foreigners is particularly low. But the whole conversation lifted me up immensely. 

Today, I sat in the park after lunch for a couple of hours, reading under the shade of a tree, listening for birds on Merlin. Today was a beautiful day - cool, not too hot, and clear. Birds were singing everywhere. Life is OK.
