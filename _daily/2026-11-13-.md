I visited two countries, today. 

I spent the majority of my time in the back of a car. To see a lot of birds, you have to see different environments, which means going to different places. It was worth it; when we stopped occasionally, I managed to see 15 new lifers today, and 20 new birds for the year. 111 birds in China so far. Numbers, like GitHub stars or points on Whose Line Is It Anyway, aren't important. But they're something. 

My main goal for the guide was to see the Chinese Merganser. This is quite literally a bird of my dreams. Around four months ago, I dreamt I was walking next to a river in China, and I saw a few of these mergansers in a small koi pond in front of a hotel there. I logged it on eBird, on a account I keep private, where I only log birds I've seen or identified in my dreams. Sometimes the birds aren't quite real - the Cupcake warbler was an interesting one -  or they're extinct - Haast eagle. But Chinese Merganser is indeed a bird. 

And today I saw a few of them, floating down a river. We share a liking for black caps, mullets, and cold water. My guide and I stood on the banks, having bushwhacked through a bamboo patch, after walking past some pretty surprised octogenarians living in very rural housing. I worried I'd get mud on my only trousers before a conference. I didn't. I also told my host that he made my dreams come true. He did. 

Besides that, other birds were good. Rustic Bunting was appropriate. I finally saw a Taiga Bean-goose, after around seven years of looking for vagrants. I also saw a Lesser White-fronted goose, which wasn't even on my radar. And parrotbills, which are adorable. 

And I saw the bullet train go a million miles an hour, leaving behind Wuyuan, as I furiously finished my slides for tomorrow. I was driven past building after building in Hangzhou. I'm writing this to you now from as fancy a hotel room as I've been in, but on the 14th floor, the kind with slippers, fluorescent lights, and a pool. There were young people in the lobby. It feels a world away from where I woke up. This room even has heating.
