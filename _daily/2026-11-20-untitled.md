I didn't feel like I got a lot of work done today, but that's possibly because I only picked up steam after working out before dinner. I cleaned the house, published a [blog post](https://www.burntfen.com/2026-11-20/new-paper-neotype-for-red-winged-blackbird) on a new paper I published both here and on [the blog for Wilson Journal of Ornithology](https://wilsonsociety.org/2026/11/19/fourteen-ways-of-looking-at-a-red-winged-blackbird/), did the laundry, ate, and talked to my sisters. Even if its hard to use the machines because I don't have the language to ask if I can have a turn easily. 

This evening I submitted two papers - one on the species *Saxicola capratus* being misparsed since the 1800s (it's pretty clearly an adjective), and one on the type species of the genus *Agelaius* (not the same as the type specimen of the species *Agelaius phoeniceus*, but you'd be forgiven for being confused). I feel good about both of these papers, but I'm learning more and more that papers on taxonomic nomenclature depend upon the editor and reviewers views more than anything else. We'll see if they get through. I wrote them months ago - I just edited them a bit, cleaned them up, and got them over the line today. 

I also submitted a paper last night, to the same journal. I have around 10 papers in review at the *Bulletin of Zoological Nomenclature*, most for over a year. I've seen reviews for one of them. It's not the fastest journal in the world. But it's the correct venue for this work, so, I submit anyway. I've applied again this year to join the Commission, where I would be able to take responsibility for the problem and hopefully help, if I get put on the slate and elected. 

It's the weekend. Going to climb the hill tomorrow, I think.
