Most of my academic publications can be found on [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5428-7535) and [Google Scholar](http://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&amp;user=X2UD62YAAAAJ). Below is a list of my publications, along with other research outputs like talks, reports, data and software. Only peer-reviewed work is shown at first; click **Peer Reviewed** to turn that filter off and see everything.

Every entry has a bibtex file, and talks have slides where I have them. Here is all of the [bibtex](https://www.burntfen.com/publications/bib/publications.bib) information at once, if you're really keen.

I'm a Topic Editor for the [Journal of Open Source Software](https://joss.theoj.org/) (JOSS), and an Associate Editor for [*Avian Systematics*](https://www.avespress.com/avian-systematics) and [*Zoological Bibliography*](https://www.avespress.com/zoological-bibliography). I'm the editor and founder of [Codex Mutabilis](https://codexmutabilis.com/) and [Wordhoard Press](https://wordhoardpress.com), which publishes original work in Old English and other extinct Germanic languages. I've reviewed for journals including *Avian Research*, *Language Policy and Language Planning*, *Literary & Linguistic Computing*, *Language Dynamics and Change*, *Début*, and JOSS; for conferences including RSAA, US-RSE, [SIGUL](http://www.elra.info/en/sig/sigul/), [SLTU-CCURL](http://sltu-ccurl-2020.ilc.cnr.it/), and ULAB; and for grants from the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation, Schmidt Futures, and the NSF. I also review code for the [U.S. Geological Survey](https://www.usgs.gov/products/software/software-management/types-software-review).
